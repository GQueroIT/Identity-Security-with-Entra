# Executive Summary

## Overview

I conducted this assessment of 52 Logistics LLC's Microsoft Entra ID environment to evaluate its current identity security posture and identify opportunities to strengthen access controls.

52 Logistics has grown significantly over the past several years and now operates with a distributed workforce, dispatchers, warehouse staff, and drivers accessing company systems from a mix of managed devices, personal devices, and locations well outside a traditional office network. As a result, identity has become the primary security boundary for anyone touching organizational resources.

Going into this engagement, leadership raised specific concerns: privileged access appeared broader than it needed to be, MFA adoption was inconsistent across the user base, no Conditional Access policies were in place, and there was no documented identity governance strategy to speak of.

My goal with this assessment is to evaluate the tenant against Zero Trust principles, identify the actual security gaps behind those concerns, determine the risk each one represents, and provide recommendations to improve authentication, authorization, privileged access management, and lifecycle governance.

The engagement covers a review of administrative roles, authentication methods, Conditional Access configurations, Identity Protection capabilities, Privileged Identity Management, and identity governance controls.

The recommendations coming out of this assessment are meant to serve as a real roadmap, one that would genuinely reduce 52 Logistics' attack surface, not just check a compliance box.
