# Executive Summary

## Overview

Acme Healthcare requested an assessment of its Microsoft Entra ID environment to evaluate its current identity security posture and identify opportunities to strengthen access controls.

The organization has experienced significant growth over the past several years and has adopted a hybrid and remote workforce model. As a result, identity has become the primary security boundary for users accessing organizational resources from unmanaged networks and devices.

Management has expressed concerns regarding excessive privileged access, inconsistent Multi-Factor Authentication adoption, the absence of Conditional Access policies, and the lack of a documented identity governance strategy.

This assessment aims to evaluate the tenant against Zero Trust principles, identify security gaps, determine associated risks, and provide recommendations to improve authentication, authorization, privileged access management, and lifecycle governance.

The engagement includes a review of administrative roles, authentication methods, Conditional Access configurations, Identity Protection capabilities, Privileged Identity Management, and identity governance controls.

Recommendations generated from this assessment will serve as a roadmap for strengthening the organization's identity security posture and reducing the overall attack surface.
