# Success Criteria

## Assessment Goals

I'm measuring success against a specific set of controls. For MFA, success means 100% of privileged accounts are protected, no exceptions. For SSPR, it means self-service password reset is enabled for all users, not just admins. For Conditional Access, it means baseline policies are actually documented, not just configured ad hoc. For PIM, success means standing Global Admin access has been minimized rather than left as permanent role assignments. For Identity Protection, it means risk policies have been reviewed and are actively in use. And for governance, it means an access review process exists and is documented, not just assumed.

---

## Technical Goals

On the technical side, I want to walk away from this engagement having documented the current state of the tenant as I found it, identified the real security gaps within it, and mapped out the risks those gaps actually represent. From there, I want to recommend concrete remediation steps, validate that the controls I implement actually work as intended, and produce operational documentation that holds up after the fact, not just screenshots with no context behind them.

---

## Business Goals

Ultimately, this project needs to do more than check boxes. I want it to genuinely reduce 52 Logistics LLC's attack surface, improve how access is managed across the organization, and support their broader move toward Zero Trust. Beyond the immediate fixes, I want to increase visibility into privileged access specifically and leave behind identity security processes that are repeatable, not one-time cleanup that drifts back to its old state six months later.
